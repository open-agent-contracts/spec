# No-Lock-In Pledge

`Open Agent Contracts` publishes the Agent Operating Contract
standard. The pledge below is the project's promise to readers,
implementers, and downstream tool authors that the standard itself
remains open and that no commercial decision the project makes later
turns adopting the standard into a tooling lock-in.

This page renders the v0 draft of the pledge. The exact wording is
gated at G-OPEN; the seven clauses below are reproduced as drafted in
the project's pledge source and may be revised before the v1.0 lock.

Public publication of this pledge to a separate portal repository is
separately gated. Nothing on this page implies that a public repository
exists today, that the pledge has been transcribed externally, or that
the v0 wording is final.

## Pledge

1. **You don't need our tools.** The Agent Operating Contract schema,
   vocabulary, gate taxonomy, and receipt envelope are documented here.
   You can implement them in any language, with any tool, for any app.
2. **The schema is the schema.** We will not add fields that only our
   tools can fill in. We will not add fields that require a paid service
   to validate.
3. **Wire-format projections stay open.** The mappings from this
   standard to MCP, WebMCP, OpenAPI, `llms.txt`, A2A, and other
   framework formats are public, documented, and do not require our
   tools.
4. **Forking is fine.** The portal source is in a public repository.
   Mirror it. Fork it. Translate it. Carry your own copy in your repo.
5. **No telemetry from the standard.** Implementing an Agent Operating
   Contract does not require pinging us. The standard does not specify
   a "registration" or "phone home" step.
6. **Premium features are tools, not specification.** If we later sell
   hosted validation, premium adapters, certification, or support, those
   are tooling and trust surfaces - not changes to the open standard.
   You can always opt out of every paid surface and remain a full
   participant in the standard.
7. **Compatibility is reported with gaps.** Our compatibility registry
   names what's covered, what's missing, and what's sharp-edged. We do
   not pretend more coverage than exists.

## Status

The pledge above is the **v0 draft** of the public pledge. Final
wording is gated at G-OPEN. Public publication and portal-repo
transcription are separately gated and have not happened. Where the
text above refers to "a public repository", the reference is to the
intended publication surface that the public repository gate covers;
no public repository exists for this portal yet.

No new clauses are introduced here. Clause-level changes happen
through the project's RFC process under the governance posture
documented on the `/about/` page.
